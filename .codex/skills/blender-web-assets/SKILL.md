# Blender assets for the web

## Establish the reference

Read the current brief and inspect the selected image before authoring. Identify the subjects, silhouettes, relative scale, depth, materials, lighting, labels and meaningful connections. An attractive render does not authorize a different composition or extra objects.

For this repository, [Design and content](../../../docs/design.md) supplies the selected references, storage conventions, material expectations and current scope. Those are project choices, not universal defaults. Read its linked authoritative briefs. Do not import historical plans from an archive.

## Author reusable geometry

Use stable asset and instance names. Build recognizable volume, thickness, curvature and surface detail that remain convincing from rotated views. An extruded outline, flat textured plane or thin logo does not establish a dimensional person, document, cloud or glass window. Reference images guide the modeling; use them as textures only when the brief explicitly calls for that technique.

Keep reusable models separate from scene instances, connections, camera tracks and screen overlays. Preserve transforms and local pivots so the same model can appear in several scenes. Build only the assets needed for the current approved example before expanding the library.

Draw the reference's meaningful connections accurately before animating their reveal or activity. Every route needs identifiable endpoints and direction. Decorative global lines must not compete with the actual relationships. Preserve route thickness, not merely its centerline, when the material depends on volume.

## Calibrate and export

Inspect a bounded render early, then front and rotated views of the highest-risk subjects. Compare matched camera, crop, resolution and lighting conditions. Measure the focal subject separately from the dark background. Region metrics and difference images supplement direct inspection; they do not establish visual approval or exact fidelity.

Export actual mesh normals, transforms, curve thickness, material energy and useful bounds. Check unit scaling on imported marks and curve extrusion. Preserve the authored camera and coordinate convention. A source material that loses depth, opacity or glow in the runtime needs further work.

When replacing or retiming an animation, inspect inherited state on both scene objects and curve datablocks. Reset or deliberately preserve their visibility, render flags and animation data. Verify the reference frame and completed state so an older reveal track cannot silently hide the new connections.

Measure export bytes, vertices and triangles by asset before browser delivery. Inspect label tessellation separately; extruded text can dominate the scene's geometry without improving readability. More polygons or thicker lettering do not establish craft. Use those measurements to spend detail on the silhouettes, materials and relationships that matter in the reference.

When a control promises full surface opacity, verify both ends of its range and the renderer's depth behavior. Keep screen-aligned explanation glass separate from dimensional app windows that rotate in the world. Use the project's selected glass source instead of approximating its style.

## Verify and persist

Verify data integrity, geometry and visual appearance as separate claims. Finite indices and a successful export do not prove recognizable anatomy, material quality, interaction or reference fidelity. Blender renders do not prove browser appearance. Respect any browser restriction and state unverified behavior plainly.

Save versioned sources, scripts, exports, renders and concise current findings in the project's designated locations. Preserve earlier assets. Record the source and tested export hashes, camera, resolution, remaining mismatch and next bounded action. Re-anchor to the current brief after a compact. Avoid chains of correction notes that retain contradictory instructions.

Use [immersive-storytelling](../immersive-storytelling/SKILL.md) when the asset work includes an explanatory scene sequence. Delegate bounded rendering only when authorized; retain a clear owner for integration and visual judgment.

# Explanatory motion

## Anchor the scene

Read the current brief and selected visual reference. For this repository, start with [Design and content](../../../docs/design.md) and its authoritative briefs. Its selected imagery, scene order, glass, access relationships and mobile behavior are project requirements, not a universal scene recipe. Archived implementations do not supply active instructions.

Identify what the viewer should understand at each meaningful pause. Follow the supplied content, not the renderer's object list. Group related details into a comprehensible step. Do not add microsteps, new capabilities or decorative connections merely because the assets exist.

## Establish continuity

Record the focal subject, starting state, transition, settled state, relationship and intended understanding. Preserve subject identity as the scene develops. When the reference represents the assembled ending, build toward it rather than beginning with every object visible. Draw the correct reference connections first, then animate their construction and directional activity.

Separate scroll-selected states, direct scrubbing, timed motion and manual inspection. An asset-review orbit is useful evidence but is not a completed scroll sequence. Reverse scrolling should reverse the intended build without losing earlier relationships. Avoid competing scene controllers.

## Compose depth and pauses

Use deliberate rotation with pan and zoom when the experience calls for a dimensional world. Reveal real geometry and parallax, rather than moving a frontal diagram across the screen. Preserve the visual anchor when approaching a spoke or related group. Keep foreground and background objects coherently oriented and scaled.

Fit semantic subjects using their actual transformed bounds and the current camera perspective. Exclude atmospheric particles from that fit. Do not reserve an empty column beneath an overlay or leave large blank regions during a pause. Preserve aspect ratio; document unavoidable framing limits rather than distorting the objects.

Screen-aligned explanation glass remains readable and fixed to its intended viewport position. Dimensional app windows remain part of the world and rotate with it. Reuse the exact selected sample components and typography. Do not invent another header, label system or numeric progress display.

Highlight the focal subject using the selected material language. Leave enough settled time to understand the step. The explanation states what matters to the intended audience and why; implementation details belong in technical records unless they help that audience decide.

## Verify the experience

Inspect opening, transition, pause and completed states, including rotated views. Check forward and reverse travel, fast scrolling, direct navigation, resize, manual interruption, keyboard input and the intended reduced-motion alternative. Scope the check to the surface being built; do not begin unrelated pages as a verification side effect.

Use matched reference comparisons for visual judgment and geometry checks for measurable invariants. A finite camera, in-bounds projection or successful render cannot establish readability, holographic fidelity or user acceptance. Verify browser presentation separately when permitted. State any access restriction and untested behavior without substituting another route around it.

Save concise current facts: the source version, tested export, actual findings, unresolved mismatch and next bounded action. Keep superseded decisions in version history. After a compact, re-read the current brief before continuing. Preserve earlier asset versions and complete the active example before using it as the template for other scenes.
